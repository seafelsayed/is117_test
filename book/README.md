# Designing Meaning: Persuasion, Archetypes, and Visual Language

This mini textbook explores how ordinary products and messages become meaningful. It follows a plain white T-shirt through three connected lenses: persuasion, brand archetypes, and visual design language. The final chapter applies those lenses to AI-assisted creative and technical work, where clear intent, bounded tasks, automated checks, and human judgment need to work together.

## How to Use This Guide

Read the chapters in order, but pause when a question or example connects to your own work. The T-shirt is intentionally ordinary: changing its presentation makes it easier to notice how audience, meaning, persuasion, and design shape interpretation. Use the questions and case study as prompts for your own analysis, then review the final synthesis before applying the framework to an AI task.
